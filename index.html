About this archive
Every spray operation published by the San Joaquin County Mosquito & Vector Control District, with the Google My Maps polygon the district drew for each one. Boundaries are the district's own; timing is as announced, not as flown.

Basemap

Basemap tiles are the one thing here that needs the network; the archive itself is local. Each background gets its own tuning: zone outlines are drawn twice, a plain white or black casing under the coloured line, and a light tint is laid over the tiles, so the outlines keep their contrast and the age fade stays readable on any of the five.
